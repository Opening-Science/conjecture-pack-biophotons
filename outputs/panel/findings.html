What run 1 on this pack found

Benchmark

The verifier benchmark, read

60 blinded items, each a scoped claim plus one verbatim sentence, drawn from the 861 rows whose stance was already adversarially verified. Refutation is deliberately over-represented (20 of 60, against roughly 9% in the corpus), so a verifier that simply agrees with the field's positivity scores badly. Sentences quoted publicly are excluded, so nobody can answer from memory.

Inter-verifier agreement: 54 of 60 items (90%).

The labels are not one model family's idiosyncrasy. They were produced by Claude judges; an independent family reproduces them at 97.5% on support and refutation and catches 19 of 20 refutations. The circularity objection is not dissolved — both are language models — but it is now a measurement rather than a worry. All substantial disagreement sits in the hedge class.

Headline

The self-preference measurement, read

This invalidates naive engine scoreboards, including the one on the Results page. The quality explanation rules itself out: if one engine simply wrote better hypotheses, both judges would rank it higher. Instead each ranks its own higher.

It generalises past this project. Every engine in the landscape verifies with the model that generated — reflection loops, simulated debate, self-scoring, data-driven reward. If a judge favours its own family by up to 40 points, a self-verified certification rate is not a measurement of quality. Cross-family verification is the minimum valid design, which is why it is wired into the harness rather than offered as an option.

Findings

What the audit caught

Fabricated attributions to real papers. Every citation existed — the mechanical stage passed 78/78 — but three judges independently found the same pattern: specific technical claims credited to works whose abstracts say nothing of the kind. An instrument specification was attributed four separate times to a paper containing no numbers at all. Existence is not support.
A hypothesis refuted by its own citation. One claimed that no photocount statistic can discriminate coherent from chaotic light, citing a paper stating that in the applicable regime they are distinguishable. Its derivations were correct; it invoked the wrong limit of its own source.
Checkable physics errors. Three hypotheses predicted 300–349 nm emission (82–95 kcal/mol) from triplet carbonyls reaching only 72–80 kcal/mol. Another proposed an estimand that is zero by construction.

Control

Evidence completeness changes verdicts, in both directions

A judge caught a defect in the harness itself: only the first eight cited abstracts were shown, while the prompt promised all of them, and 28% of hypotheses cite more. Re-running with complete evidence turned the accident into a controlled comparison — same candidates, same prompt, same judges, only the evidence differs.

Corpus quality

What the run found in the corpus

The field's epistemic anchor has no abstract. The critical review of photocount methodology that the field treats as its quality benchmark is cited by seven candidates and holds no abstract in the knowledgebase, so it frames the run sight-unseen. A judge named it unprompted as the highest-value abstract to acquire.

118 works (1.0%) carry abstracts sharing no significant term with their title: publisher boilerplate, non-English text, truncations, and at least one wrong-record join. 70 of 516 inlined citations had no usable abstract, now marked explicitly so a corpus gap is not read as a fabricated citation.

Caveats

Limits

Two engines, two judge families, 78 candidates, one field. The direction of the self-preference effect is consistent and the Codex magnitude far too large to be noise, but these are not calibrated constants. Certified means the reasoning survived audit; it does not mean true, and nothing here has been tested at a bench.
